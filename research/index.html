RESEARCH

Dr. Zhang's research explores the design of cryptographic mechanisms and their use in securing data, computation, and intelligent systems. The research program spans four complementary areas:

  • Public-key and applied cryptography: functional and witness encryption, identity-based encryption, leakage resilience, and provably secure program obfuscation.
  • Big-data security and privacy: secure data sharing, verifiable statistics, privacy-preserving aggregation, and outsourced computation.
  • Blockchain and industrial Internet security: blockchain supervision, secure crowdsensing, fair electronic auctions, and privacy in the Internet of Things.
  • Artificial intelligence security: privacy-preserving federated learning, secure model evaluation, and protected computation on sensitive datasets.
