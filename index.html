Mingwu ZHANG

Professor of Computer Science
Ph.D. and Master's Supervisor
Director, Institute of Cyberspace Security
School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence
Hubei University of Technology, Wuhan, China

Dr. Mingwu Zhang is a Professor at the School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Hubei University of Technology (HBUT). He directs the university's Institute of Cyberspace Security. His research focuses on public-key and applied cryptography, big-data security and privacy, blockchain and industrial Internet security, and artificial intelligence security.

Dr. Zhang is a Hubei Province distinguished talent and was selected as a Chutian Scholar Distinguished Professor in 2013. He has been included in the career-long list of the world's top 2% scientists. From 2010 to 2012, he was a postdoctoral researcher and JSPS Fellow at Kyushu University, Japan. He was a visiting scholar at the University of Wollongong, Australia, from 2015 to 2016.

His research connects the foundations of cryptography with privacy-sensitive applications. Topics include functional encryption, witness encryption, leakage-resilient cryptography, provably secure program obfuscation, privacy-preserving machine learning, and secure data sharing. He has led more than 30 research projects, including projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, provincial research programs, JSPS, and industry.

He has published more than 100 research papers in journals and conferences including Designs, Codes and Cryptography, IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security, IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing, IEEE Transactions on Services Computing, and ASIACRYPT. He has authored three research monographs and served as an author or editor of six textbooks.

Dr. Zhang's work has received eight best paper awards, including recognition at ACISP 2018, Inscrypt 2018, EIDWT 2017, and FCS 2024. His academic service includes editorial roles for more than ten journals and conference leadership at ProvSec 2023, EIDWT 2017, AsiaJCIS 2014, and JWIS 2010.

He also serves as Director of the Hubei Engineering Technology Research Center for SME Big Data and Deputy Director of the Hubei Blockchain Technology Innovation Research Institute.
